      Method and sources

      Lightning side: SLDS 2 (Summer '26, 3.3.1) components index read live in the browser on 2026-09-16: a flat list of 48. SLDS 2 pages are usage-level (overview, anatomy, states, interactions) and their side navigation is not exposed in the DOM, so pages were reached by URL slug; six were read in full (Vertical Navigation, Tree, Tree Grid, Progress Indicator, Combobox, Prompt). Variant inventories come from the SLDS 1 blueprints (v1.lightningdesignsystem.com, 16 pages), which SLDS 2 still links as related components. The ten CRM patterns exist only in SLDS 1 and are an appendix, not part of the 48. The SLDS 2 Patterns section could not be indexed.

      DS side: the census of 2026-09-15 (masters unchanged). Evidence: the product carries a pipeline concept (the CE "Seleziona pipeline" pattern, rebuilt as Input + Dropdown), the natural home of Path.
