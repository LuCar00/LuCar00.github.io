Evidence × effort — where each candidate sits

The 25 candidates (5 gaps, 14 near extensions, 6 out-of-scope items) placed by how strong the evidence of need is and how much they cost. Effort is in waves, our usual unit; one wave is a mockup passage plus a master plus QA and records. Top-left is the natural starting point.

Evidence: proven = already used in the working file · basic = a standard control or a pattern the CRM screens hit constantly · conditional = depends on a roadmap decision · none = no CRM evidence. Effort: ⅛ = props-only extension · ¼ = small master or variant axis · ½ = master + sub-component or a published-master restructure · 1 = a family.

      Method and sources

      DSI side: the 55 components of the official index (designers.italia.it/design-system/componenti, read 2026-09-15) plus the detail pages of the 16 whose scope is not obvious from the name (bottomnav, back, forward, dimmer, thumbnav, transfer, navscroll, sticky, affix, toolbar, sections, notifications, callout, timeline, steppers, rating). Bootstrap Italia semantics for the rest. For the 14 "near" components, the official variants and anatomy lists of each DSI page were read the same day and turned one-to-one into the feature rows of the deep-dive.

      DS side: live census of every component set and standalone component per page: Actions 7 sets + 3 · Forms 10 + 3 · Navigation 6 + 1 · Feedback & status 9 + 1 · Cards & patterns 8 + 6 · AI 10 + 2 · Data display 16 + 14 · Utilities 1 + 3; icon pages counted only (1,545 Line Awesome, 24 Property). WIP - Marco holds two local _Preview/* sets mirroring published masters; WIP - Luca holds no masters. Variant axes and property names were read from the masters, not from the registry.

      Evidence check: the CE and NDL censuses of the working file were searched for the missing components. Only Pagination appears, as the local master 13964:178789 with 31 instances. No radio, accordion, bottom nav, tab bar, timeline or rating came from the old libraries.

      Limits: the comparison is by role and function, not variant by variant; DSI variant sets (for example ~20 Card variants) were mapped to the DS master that covers the role. Role groups are mine. Effort figures are estimates in waves under the standard pipeline (mockup → Vai → master → QA strip → records). Companion document: ds-next-claude/DSI-COMPARISON.md.
